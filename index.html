Common Questions
FAQ
Do I need to book an appointment? +

Walk-ins are welcome, but we recommend booking in advance especially for bridal, keratin, and special services to avoid waiting. Call or WhatsApp us to book!

What are your working hours? +

We are open daily from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM at both our branches in Dhayari Phata and Nanded Fata, Pune.

Do you offer bridal trial sessions? +

Yes! We offer pre-bridal trial sessions at ₹2,500 so you can finalize your look before the big day. Highly recommended for all brides!

Which products do you use? +

We use premium brands like L'Oréal Professionnel, Wella, O3+, and Kryolan for makeup — ensuring the best results and skin safety for all our clients.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept Cash and all UPI payments (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm). No card payments at the moment.

Is Riya Beauty Salon only for ladies? +

Yes, Riya Beauty Salon is exclusively for ladies. We provide a comfortable, private, and relaxing environment for all our female clients.
